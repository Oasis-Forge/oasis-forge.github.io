# Sundown privacy policy

Publisher: Oasis Forge.
Last updated: 23 November 2026.
Privacy contact: oasisforge.support@gmail.com.

Sundown is a single-player survival game. It has no account of its own. Your progress and settings are kept on your device. A few Google services work with the game. This page says what each one does and what it receives.

## What stays on your device

These are kept in the app's own storage on your device:

- game saves and settings
- your list of purchases (product ids only, no card details)
- your answer to the "Help improve Sundown?" card
- the daily reward record

Oasis Forge runs no server and receives none of these files. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes them, along with your local progress.

## Services that run for the game to work

These start without a question, because the game needs them. Google handles the data they use; Oasis Forge does not receive it. Google Play Games also sends Google device and app identifiers and diagnostics, under Google's privacy policy.

- **Google Play Billing** (Google Play): lets you buy optional items and restores them on a new phone (the Restore purchases button in the Store and in Settings). The items are Map 2, the Supporter pack, outfits and weapon finishes. Google handles your payment and purchase history under Google's own privacy policy. The game stores only which products you own.
- **Google Play Games**: signs you in automatically when you are signed in to Play Games on the phone, and records achievements. If you are not signed in, the game plays fully offline. Settings shows whether you are signed in and has a Sign in button.
- **Cloud saves** (Google Play Games, Saved Games): your save is copied to your Google account so you can continue on another phone. The save holds your progress: run id, day, map, play time, save timestamps, items and story state. Google stores it, not Oasis Forge.

## Optional: crash reports and play stats

On first launch the game asks "Help improve Sundown? Send anonymous crash reports and play stats." The answer is **No** unless you tap Yes. You can change the two switches (crash reports, play stats) at any time in Settings > Privacy.

If you say Yes, **Firebase Crashlytics** (crash reports) and **Google Analytics for Firebase** (play stats) from Google receive:

- crash and error details, with the phone model, Android version and app version;
- a random app-install id (not your name, email or Google account);
- anonymous play events: `session_start`, and `consent_changed` (sent when you change this setting). More gameplay events may be added; this page is updated when they are.

We use them to fix crashes and balance the game. The game sends no free text, no names and no advertising id to these services. Google processes the data for us under Google's terms. If you say No, or switch both off, collection stops.

Firebase is part of the app and starts with collection switched off. It may contact Google to set itself up, but it sends no crash reports or play stats until you tap Yes.

## Optional: rewarded ads

The only ad in the game is on the home radio. "Supply drop" lets you choose to watch a short ad for a small in-game crate. There are no other ads and none are forced.

Nothing is loaded and no ad request is made until you tap **Watch ad**. Then **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads) loads an ad.

**Google's consent form:** before the ad, Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) may appear. It appears only where the law requires it, such as the EEA and the UK. It lets you accept or refuse personalised ads.

**What Google receives:** to show the ad, Google receives data from your phone:

- your device's advertising id
- your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it
- device and app details
- whether you watched or skipped the ad

Google uses this data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

**Personalised ads:** in the regions where the consent form applies, personalised ads are used only if you agree in it. Elsewhere, ads may be personalised.

**Changing your choice:**

- In **Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices**, at any time. The row appears once Google's form applies to you.
- In Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads), in every region: opt out of ads personalisation, or reset or delete your advertising id.

**Who controls the data:** Google is a separate controller of the data it collects for ads. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Your consent choice is stored by Google's consent library on your device; Oasis Forge does not receive it.

## Feedback by email

If you email us, we receive your message and address. We use them to answer you and improve the game. Tell us if you want them deleted.

## Children

Sundown contains horror and blood and is for ages 16 and up. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. Ads are requested with a Teen content rating, and the game does not ask Google to treat any user as a child.

## Deleting your data

- **Local data:** clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall.
- **Cloud save and achievements:** delete them in the Play Games app's settings (your Google account), or write to us.
- **Crash reports and play stats:** write to oasisforge.support@gmail.com. Switching both off stops all further collection.
- **Purchases:** Google keeps the purchase record; ask Google Play.

## Changes

If we add a new kind of data collection or a new service, we will update this page before it ships. If it is not needed for the game to work, we will ask you first.
