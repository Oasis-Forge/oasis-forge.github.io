# Sundown privacy policy

Publisher: Oasis Forge.
Last updated: 7 November 2026.
Privacy contact: oasisforge.support@gmail.com.

## Current build

Sundown is an offline, single-player game. It has no account or sign-in. Game saves and settings stay on your device. Oasis Forge does not collect or share personal data through this build.

There are no in-game ads or purchases yet. Analytics and automatic crash reporting are not included in the current build.

## Testing on Google Play

If you join a Google Play test, Google handles your opt-in and the app's install. If you send us feedback by email, we receive your message and your email address, and use them only to answer you and improve the game. Tell us if you want your message deleted.

## Local saves

The game keeps progress and settings on your device so you can continue playing. Oasis Forge does not receive these files and has no server copy to retain or delete. You can remove local game data through Android's app storage settings. This removes your progress.

## Age

Sundown contains horror and blood and is intended for ages 16 and up. It is not directed at children.

## Future changes

If analytics or crash reports are added, they will be opt-in. You will be asked before they are enabled. This policy will explain the data, purpose, service providers, retention and deletion options before collection starts.

This privacy policy will be updated before ads or purchases ship. It will also be updated before any new kind of data collection or cloud save is added.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact Oasis Forge at oasisforge.support@gmail.com.
