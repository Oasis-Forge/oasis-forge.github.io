# Privacy policy

**Territory Conquest**, by Oasis Forge

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what information Territory Conquest ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. In short:

- Games against bots and your progress stay on your phone.
- Playing with friends online uses a guest account with the name you choose, and shows your public look to the
  players in your room.
- There are no ads, analytics or tracking.

The game is for players aged 18 and over.

## What stays on your phone

These are saved only on your phone, and we never receive a copy:

- your XP, badges, goals and stats;
- match history and campaign progress;
- a record of what you bought;
- your settings;
- a saved game against bots;
- a log of recent errors, which stays on your phone unless you copy it yourself (for example to send us a bug
  report).

Uninstalling the game, or choosing **Reset local data** in Settings, deletes them.

## Playing with friends online

When you play with friends, the game uses:

- **A guest account** created by our sign-in provider, Supabase. It holds an account id, the display name you chose,
  and the fact that you confirmed you are 18 or over.
- **Your public look**: the crest, frame, color, title, pinned badge and level you chose. It is sent to the game
  server and shown to the other players in your room.
- **Game messages** sent to our game server: your moves, emotes and room actions, the room code, your account id and
  your name. The server keeps each room, and also saves it to its own disk so a restart does not end your game. This
  includes the game itself, the players' account ids, names and public looks. A room is kept after a game ends so
  players can see the results and start a rematch, and is deleted 7 days after its last player disconnects. There is
  no free-text chat.
- **Connection data** such as your IP address and the time you connect. Supabase and our server host, Oracle Cloud,
  see this when you connect, as with any internet service.

If you choose email sign-in (when it is offered), Supabase also keeps your email address.

## Blocking and reporting

- **Blocking** is kept only on your phone (the list of people you blocked, their account ids and the names they had).
  It hides their emotes and name on your phone. When you search for a quick match, the account ids of the people you
  blocked are sent to the game server for that search, so you are not seated together; the server does not keep them.
- **Reporting** sends a report to us. A report contains: the time, your account id and name, the account id and name
  of the player you report, the reason you pick (offensive name, harassment, cheating or something else), the short
  note you may add (up to 200 characters) and the room code. Only we see reports (through the game server's log and
  a file on the server). We use them to review and remove abusive players, and we keep them for 90 days, then they
  are deleted.

## What we don't collect

The game does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It does not use an advertising id,
analytics, crash-reporting services or tracking. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and the in-game
Privacy page first.

## Why we use it

To sign you in, put you in a room with your friends, run fair games on the server, and keep the service safe (for
example, reviewing reports and removing abusive players).

## Sharing

We do not sell your data. Supabase (sign-in) and Oracle Cloud (game server) process it for us. We may disclose data
if the law requires it.

## Deleting your data

- Delete your online account at any time in the game: **Settings**, **Account and data**, **Delete account**. This
  removes your account id, name and 18+ confirmation from our sign-in provider.
- Erase what is on your phone with **Reset local data** in the same place, or by uninstalling the game.
- If you can't use the game, email us (below) and we will delete your account.

## Children

The game is not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If we learn that an
account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you can ask us to access, correct or delete
your data. Email us (below).

## Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.
