Read the privacy policy

Your offline progress stays with you.

Games against bots, campaign progress, stats and settings stay on your phone. Offline play needs no sign-in.

Online play, clearly explained.

A guest account holds your chosen name and 18+ confirmation. Your public look is shown to your room; moves and preset emotes go through the game server. There’s no free-text chat.

Your data. Your controls.

Delete your online account in Settings, under Account and data. Reset local data to erase what’s on your phone. The privacy policy explains storage, service providers and your choices.
