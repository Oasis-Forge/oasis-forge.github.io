Read the privacy policy
01

On your phone.

No account or sign-in. Scans, created codes and history are stored on your device. Link checks run locally, without sending the scanned address to a server.

Android may include stored app data in your device backup if you have it enabled.

02

Space to do the job.

No ads over the camera, scan results or code editor. The free app includes the core tools; removing ads is one optional, one-time purchase.

Banners appear in History, Settings and the Create picker. A full-screen ad may follow saving or sharing a created code.

03

Understand what’s shared.

Camera images and imported photos aren’t stored. Google’s scanner sends usage and performance statistics; ads can involve device and interaction data.

The privacy policy explains these services, permissions and your ad choices.
