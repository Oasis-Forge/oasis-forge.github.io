# Privacy policy

**Pop Rivals**, by Oasis Forge

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains what information Pop Rivals ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The short version: your game progress stays on your phone, we do not run any servers, and we do not collect personal information. The only outside services are Google's ad network and the app store you buy from.

## What stays on your phone

Your progress is saved only on your phone: levels, stars, coins, hearts, power-ups, skins, daily tasks, your daily streak, a recording of your best race on each level (for "Beat your best") and your settings. We never receive a copy of it. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, your progress is deleted from the phone. If Android's own backup is switched on, Android may keep a copy of the game's save in your Google account's backup and put it back when you install the game again. That backup is kept by Google for you; we cannot see it.

The game does not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services. It does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or microphone.

## Feedback

If you choose **Send feedback** in Settings, your own email app opens with a message to us. It already contains the game's version, your phone's model and Android version, and how many races you played and won on each level, so we can see where the game is too hard or too easy. You can read, change or delete any of it before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. We use what you send only to reply and to improve the game, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

## Ads

The game is free and shows ads through **Google AdMob**. Some ads you choose to watch for a reward (for example an extra heart). Others appear between races, never during one.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, and how you interact with the ads. Google uses this information as described in its own policies:

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under **Settings, Privacy choices** (shown where it applies). You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings.

If you buy **Remove ads**, ads between races stop. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available, because they are your choice.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by **Google Play** (or the **Apple App Store** on iPhone). We never see your payment details. The store tells the game which items you own so it can unlock them, and "Restore purchases" in Settings asks the store again. The store's own privacy policy applies to purchases:

- Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules a few reminders on your phone, for example when your hearts are full again. They are created on your phone; no server sends them. You can turn them off under **Settings, Reminders**, or in your phone's settings.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested with settings that are not targeted at children.

## Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal information, there is no account or personal data held by us to access, correct or delete. Your progress can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the game or by choosing **Start over from level 1** in Settings. If you have emailed us, you can ask us at the address below to delete your messages. For information Google holds in connection with ads, use the links above or your Google account settings.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new "Last updated" date, and the game will include it.
