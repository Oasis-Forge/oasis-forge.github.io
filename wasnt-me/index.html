What happens in the clip?

The cat sneaks across the flat, takes the roast chicken and carries it toward its bed. It sits still when the human looks, then continues the heist. The clip has no spoken dialogue.

Your progress stays with you.

Pets, settings and progress are stored on your device. Friends' scores are optional, off by default and available only to players 16 and over.

A few grown-up details.

The game includes ads and in-app purchases. Ads are non-personalized for everyone, and purchases ask a grown-up's question first. Google and Unity handle some technical and purchase data; the policy explains what is shared.
