Read the privacy policy

Your progress stays on your device.

No account is needed to play. Friends’ scores are off until you turn them on, and available only to players 16 and over. Enabling them sends results, replays, your character’s look and a friend code to Unity.

Ads and purchases, explained.

Dust Devil has ads and optional in-app purchases. Ads are set up for children for everyone, with personalization off. Purchases ask a grown-up’s question first. Google and Unity receive technical information for ads, purchases and engine services; the privacy policy has the details.
