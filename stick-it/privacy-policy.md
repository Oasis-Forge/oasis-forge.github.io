# Privacy policy

**Stick It**, by Oasis Forge

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains what information Stick It ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. In short:

- Your game progress stays on your phone.
- We do not run any servers.
- We do not collect personal information.

The only outside services are Google's ad network and Google Play.

## What stays on your phone

Your progress is saved only on your phone. That includes:

- levels and stars;
- coins and hearts;
- power-ups and skins;
- daily tasks, your daily streak and your daily wheel results;
- your settings.

We never receive a copy of it. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, your progress is deleted.

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include your saved progress in your phone's backup to your Google account, as it does for other apps. That lets a new phone or a reinstall pick up where you left off. The backup is handled by Google under its privacy policy, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings. We never see it.

The game does not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services. It does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.

## Sharing your daily wheel result

When you tap **Share** on a daily wheel result, the game makes a picture of your result on your phone. It then opens your phone's share menu, so you choose the app and the people to send it to. The game sends nothing unless you do this.

## Ads

The game is free and shows ads through **Google AdMob**.

- **Ads you choose to watch:** for a reward, for example an extra heart, a continue after a miss, double coins or a free power-up.
- **Ads between levels:** these sometimes appear after a win, never during play.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, including:

- your device's advertising ID;
- your IP address;
- your device model and operating system;
- how you interact with the ads.

Google uses this information as described in its own policies:

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. This applies, for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states. You can change your choice at any time in the game under **Settings, Privacy options**, which is shown where it applies. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings.

If you buy **Remove ads** or the **Starter pack**, the ads between levels stop. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available, because they are your choice.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by **Google Play**, and we never see your payment details.

- Google Play tells the game which items you own, so it can unlock them.
- **Restore purchases** in Settings asks Google Play again.
- If the game asks you to rate it, that request is shown by Google Play too.

Google Play's privacy policy applies to purchases and ratings: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules a few reminders on your phone: when your hearts are full again, when your daily tasks are ready, and when your streak is about to end. The reminders are created on your phone; no server sends them. You can turn them off under **Settings, Reminders**, or in your phone's settings.

## Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

## Your rights

We do not collect or store personal information, so we hold no account or personal data for you to access, correct or delete.

- You can delete your progress at any time by uninstalling the game or by choosing **Reset progress** in Settings.
- For information Google holds in connection with ads, backups or purchases, use the links above or your Google account settings.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new "Last updated" date, and the game will include it.
